# Dots and Boxes

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/dots-and-boxes/)

https://libyan11.github.io/dots-and-boxes/

Dots and Boxes in your browser, on phone or computer. Play online with up to 4 friends, or against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard. Works offline after the first visit. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Play with friends:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. Everyone else types the code and taps *Join room*. 2 to 4 players; the host can fill seats with computer players.

**Rules**
- Take turns drawing a line between two neighbouring dots. Tap near a line to pick it, tap again to draw it.
- Draw the fourth side of a box to claim it, then go again.
- When every box is taken, most boxes wins.
- Boards: Quick 3×3, Classic 5×5, Big 7×7.
